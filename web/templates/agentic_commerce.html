🤖 Agentic Commerce

Evaluates your Magento catalog as machine-consumable commerce data — the structured facts an AI shopping agent needs to filter, compare, recommend and buy your products (age, material, learning intent, personalization, price, availability). It reads Magento, reconciles against your storefront JSON-LD and product feed, scores each product's agent-readiness, and tests real shopper questions. Read-only — it never changes the catalog and never invents a fact; a missing field is reported as a gap, not filled in.

Reads the full catalog from Magento, then reconciles the top products by demand against storefront JSON-LD + feed (~1 req/sec) — runs in the background.
