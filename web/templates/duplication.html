🧬 Duplicate & Thin-Variation Pages

Finds pages that repeat the same passages — templated "X for [age/type]" pages that share sentences with a word swapped. It measures shared {{ '' }}8-word sequences across the full live page content (nav/header/footer stripped), which is how near-duplicate detection actually works — not shared vocabulary, which every page on the same topic has. Google dampens clusters of near-duplicate pages; consolidating them concentrates the value onto one strong page.

Honest calibration: genuinely distinct same-topic pages share ~0–3% of their passages; above ~12–15% means real templated/repeated content. This is a standard near-dup method (n-gram shingling), close to but not identical to Google's proprietary system — treat clusters as strong leads, and confirm a fix by consolidating and measuring position in GSC over ~4 weeks. The scan fetches live pages, so it takes a few minutes.
