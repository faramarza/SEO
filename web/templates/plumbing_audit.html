🔧 Plumbing Audit

A read-only technical-SEO audit of where Google's crawl and indexing are being wasted on the Magento store — parameter/filter junk, canonicals, and internal linking. It crawls the live site politely (~1 request/sec, capped) and reads GSC + the sitemap + robots. It never changes anything — every fix is a proposal with where it lives in Magento and its risk. Impact is measured (counts + junk ratio), never invented.

A full run crawls up to ~1,200 URLs at 1/sec (≈20 min) plus a GSC URL-inspection sample — it runs in the background; results and CSV baselines save when it finishes.
