Method: Striking Distance (Dean / Patel). Queries where a page already ranks just off page 1 (positions 6–20) with real search demand. Method: Content Decay → Refresh (Patel / Dean). Pages that once earned traffic and have since slid. Refreshing decaying content (update facts, deepen to match current competitors, realign intent, refresh internal links) is one of the highest-ROI SEO moves — Backlinko saw +260% in 14 days from a refresh.

Method: Content Pruning (Dean). Dead-weight pages — no traffic, no demand, thin and/or orphaned — dilute your site's topical focus.
