Method: CTR Recovery. Your biggest measurable leak: pages that already rank on page 1 but get clicked below a healthy, achievable CTR for that position (a normal listing at #3–5 earns 7–11%). The ranking and impressions already exist — recovering the clicks needs no new authority, just a title/meta that earns the click (and, where it helps, star ratings showing in the result). Each row shows the lost clicks (and revenue at your measured conversion rate) and a grounded reason. Hit Rewrite for title/meta options.

Method: CRO Leak Finder. Money pages (products, categories) that already get traffic but convert below your own average for their type — ranked by recoverable revenue, so the top of the list is the most money, not just the worst rate. Benchmarks are your own GA4 conversion rates, and each reason is grounded in the real page (thin copy, missing reviews/price, high bounce). Method: Striking Distance (Dean / Patel). Queries where a page already ranks just off page 1 (positions 6–20) with real search demand. These are the cheapest ranking wins — the page is already relevant, it just needs a nudge (tighter on-page relevance for the query + internal links from strong pages) to cross onto page 1 where clicks happen.

Method: Content Decay → Refresh (Patel / Dean). Pages that once earned traffic and have since slid. Refreshing decaying content (update facts, deepen to match current competitors, realign intent, refresh internal links) is one of the highest-ROI SEO moves — Backlinko saw +260% in 14 days from a refresh.

Method: Content Pruning (Dean). Dead-weight pages — no traffic, no demand, thin and/or orphaned — dilute your site's topical focus. Merge/redirect them into a stronger related page, or prune (noindex/remove). Review candidates only — products and category pages are never flagged, and nothing is changed automatically.

Method: Reviews Priority. Reviews are the highest-leverage trust investment for a respectable brand — stars lift click-through, on-page reviews lift conversion, and the rating schema is the gap Rich Results flags. You can't collect reviews everywhere at once, so this ranks the products with no review schema by the real demand and revenue already flowing through them — run your review-collection campaign top-down. No invented uplift numbers; the priority is your own measured traffic and revenue.

Method: Rich Results / Structured Data. Star ratings, price & availability, FAQ accordions and breadcrumbs in the SERP are the one CTR advantage you can win without domain authority — and the same schema makes pages quotable by AI answer engines. This reads the JSON-LD each page already emits (Magento outputs a lot by default), confirms coverage, and lists only the genuinely missing types with ready-to-paste markup grounded in the page's real data. It never asks you to add schema you already have, and never fabricates ratings — AggregateRating is flagged as an opportunity only once you can back it with real reviews.

Method: GEO — Generative Engine Optimization (Török 2026 / Pinterest 2025). AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) cite sources rather than rank links. Citation is driven by evidence (stats/data), structure (headings, lists, tables), answerability (question-form content + FAQ schema), and freshness. This scores each page's citation readiness and gives concrete fixes. Emerging best practice — treat as directional guidance. Use AI Questions to generate the questions shoppers ask an AI (Pinterest "reverse search") with ready-to-paste FAQ answers.

Method: Brand SERP + Merchant readiness. Two realistic presence checks. Brand SERP: do you own your own name in search? Branded queries where you're not solidly #1 or where click-through is oddly low mean demand that's rightfully yours is leaking to resellers, marketplaces, or ads. Merchant readiness: products can show in Google's free product listings only if the page carries Product + Offer structured data — this flags those that aren't feed-ready, by traffic.

Method: Paid-Search Relevance Auditor (Walmart AdTech / Rokon 2025). Walmart fine-tuned an LLM to classify whether a search query is relevant to an ad. This runs the actionable core: it pulls your Google Ads search-term report, classifies each term's relevance to what you actually sell (relevant / partial / irrelevant), and flags irrelevant terms that spent money without converting as negative-keyword candidates — surfacing wasted ad spend. Live + AI-classified, so run it on demand.
Method: GSC-driven link prospecting. Your winnable pages (position 4–20, non-brand demand, ~0 clicks) plus the Striking Distance rows marked Needs backlinks are the URLs where external authority is the remaining lever. For each, this finds SHOULD-GET prospects (topically visible resource/roundup pages, via Serper) and CAN-GET prospects (domains that demonstrably link to the content sites outranking you, via DataForSEO), then ranks them by a balanced blend of Impact × Acquisition probability — a great score on one axis can't hide a terrible one. Every prospect shows its factual evidence. Domains already linking to you are excluded automatically.
